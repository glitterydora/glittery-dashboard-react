LD-8: Deliver a multilingual and accessible Economy experience

User story
As a Lufthansa passenger, I want the Onboard Concierge to work in my language and with assistive technology.

Acceptance criteria
- Support German, English and French for core flows and messages.
- Provide accessible labels, focus order, contrast and error states.
- Localize dates, times, currencies and airport names.
- Validate mobile web and app journeys with accessibility QA.

Dependencies
Content design, translation, accessibility QA and partner UI constraints.
