LD-2: Book Deutsche Bahn or SNCF rail travel after landing

User story
As an Economy passenger, I want to book onward rail travel after landing.

Acceptance criteria
- Show eligible Deutsche Bahn or SNCF options using arrival data.
- Present departure time, duration, class, price and conditions.
- Add a successful booking to the trip view.
- Explain unavailable inventory or partner errors clearly.

Dependencies
Flight status, Deutsche Bahn and SNCF APIs, payments and localization.
