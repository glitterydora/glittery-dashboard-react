LD-7: Send contextual reminders and service notifications

User story
As an Economy passenger, I want timely reminders about my selected services.

Acceptance criteria
- Remind passengers about meal selection deadlines.
- Send rail, taxi and duty-free confirmations or disruption updates.
- Deep-link each notification to the relevant trip service.
- Respect notification preferences and quiet hours.

Dependencies
Flight status, partner webhooks, notification service, consent and localization.
